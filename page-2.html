Who We Are

Fabrication built around precision, not shortcuts

Zoom Steel Aluminium designs, manufactures and installs steel and aluminium work for homes, offices and factories across Mumbai. Every gate, railing and window passes through the same process: measured on site, drawn to scale, cut and welded in our own workshop, then finished and installed by the team that built it.

  • Modern fabrication — CNC-cut sections and jig-welded joints for consistent, straight lines.
  • Skilled in-house team — designers, welders and fitters who work on your project start to finish.
  • Quality materials — certified MS, SS and aluminium sections, toughened glass only.
  • Timely completion — a fixed install date, confirmed before work begins.
