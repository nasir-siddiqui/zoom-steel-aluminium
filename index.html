About the Workshop

Precision fabrication, measured on site

Zoom Steel Aluminium specialises in high-quality steel and aluminium fabrication for residential, commercial and industrial projects. We design, manufacture and install custom gates, railings, safety grills, aluminium windows, toughened glass partitions, staircases, sliding doors and other fabrication solutions.

Our focus stays on durability, precision craftsmanship, modern design and timely completion — whether it's a new construction or a renovation, every job is built to your site's exact measurements.

How a Project Runs
01

Site Visit & Measurement

We visit and take exact measurements for your gate, railing or window opening.

02

Custom Design

A design is worked out with you — material, finish and style confirmed before fabrication.

03

Fabrication

Cutting, welding and finishing done in the workshop to the agreed spec.

04

On-Site Installation

Delivered and fitted at your home, office or site, ready for use.
